# BI Trade product interface

- This is a product for users. Show only information that helps them understand a strategy, their account, positions, fills, fees, profit/loss, or an action they need to take.
- Do not display AI providers or model names, connection progress, infrastructure brands, internal request/database budgets, debugging output, exception text, or recovery plumbing on public pages. Keep diagnostics in private server records.
- When a user must act, explain the practical impact and next step in plain language. Do not replace an actual service failure with an invented successful decision or trade.
- Preserve the real timestamp of the last valid evaluation. Retained data must not look newly evaluated. Keep financial losses, unsuccessful attempts and unknown risk evidence truthful; use concise user-facing language and details where appropriate.
- Preserve the existing fog-white/deep-blue palette, light/dark themes, Chinese/English support, mobile usability and motion controls.
- Trading remains simulated. Do not enable real-money signing/broadcast, deposits/withdrawals, token issuance or buybacks, forced trades, fixed position caps, or new fixed take-profit/stop-loss rules without the user's instruction.
- Do not purchase services or upgrade plans. Do not reset the current VPS ledger or delete the old Cloudflare archive. The earlier full fresh start was a completed one-time migration choice, not ongoing reset authorization.
