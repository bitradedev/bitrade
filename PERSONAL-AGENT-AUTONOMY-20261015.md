# Personal Agent strategy autonomy · 2026-10-15

The user reported a personal Agent's candidate being refused for holder concentration and requested that external Agents be allowed to choose their own strategies.

The root was the platform buy guard: in Meme mode it applied the complete built-in screening report to every Agent. Concentration, holder count, turnover, related-wallet thresholds and missing screening evidence therefore vetoed personal strategies. PaperDesk also applied the built-in 2% entry-price-impact ceiling globally.

For external paper Agents only, built-in strategy screening and the 2% impact veto are removed at preparation and later matching. The platform's three built-ins keep their original screening and price-impact policy. `get_account.result.paperTradingPolicy` identifies the policy to integrations, including a null personal impact cap and the existing 3,000 basis-point later-block slippage boundary. Null is not zero impact. Personal Agents still run their own strategy externally; no arbitrary AI decisions are manufactured by the website.

An external Agent can submit a supported BSC Meme CA outside the twelve-token built-in discovery pool. The chain's Flap Portal or Four.meme helper must establish launch identity before metadata and real prices are acquired on demand. Personal identities are persisted separately for reconstruction and held exits. They are neither an active built-in candidate nor a passing risk report. Existing Flap routes and graduated Four.meme routes are supported; unsupported Four.meme curves, extension/tax/conversion routes, missing identities and unrelated assets cannot acquire fabricated quotes. Quote-asset sellability/tax checks needed for correct route arithmetic remain.

Credentials and account isolation, pauses/closure, caller-authorized account policy, available balance including fees, known owned sell amounts, actual fresh fixed-block quotes and full reverse quotes, exact recipients, reorg checks, explicit minimum output and 30% later-block slippage remain. No fixed personal order-size cap, new exit rule or force-trade loop is added. No real signature/broadcast is enabled. Existing failed requestKeys keep their prior result; a genuinely new evaluated intent needs a new key. Historical requests, losses and risk evidence are retained, and unknown evidence is not changed to a clean check.

Validation: 111 product checks, 150 Cloudflare checks and six VPS checks pass. The product case uses a blocked concentration report and a 20% buy-impact quote: a personal 500U buy enters matching and fills in an isolated ledger while the same built-in screening still refuses entry. Other existing cases retain pause/authentication/overspend/wrong-recipient/30%-slippage protections. A real quote-adapter fixture verifies an undiscovered Flap curve with over-2% impact, actual later-block simulation and balanced ledger; a Four.meme case verifies on-demand origin outside discovery, reconstruction support, no built-in eligibility and refusal of unrelated assets. These are controlled tests, not production fills or proof of profitability. Core formatting, client syntax and VPS build pass; exact-release CI and production rollout follow below.
